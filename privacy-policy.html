Yarn Dash — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Yarn Dash is a game made for children and families. We designed it so that we, the developer, do not collect any personal information about you or your child.

What the game stores

Your progress (level, credits, stars, Cat House decorations, unlocked cats and yarns, settings and scarves) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which is certified for Google Play's Families program. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and limited to content rated G, so ads are not personalized. The game does not use the device's advertising identifier. Google may still process limited device information (such as IP address and app interaction data) to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Some ads are optional videos that give an in-game reward. The player always chooses whether to watch them. Players who buy "No Ads" stop seeing ads between levels.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store payment details. Every purchase in the game is protected by a grown-up check, and Google Play's own parental controls also apply.

Reminders

If you allow it, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example "your lives are full"). They are created on the device itself, no information is sent anywhere, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings at any time.

No accounts, chat or sharing

The game has no sign-in, no chat, no friends list and no way to share personal information with other players.

Children's privacy

We comply with the requirements of Google Play's Families policy and do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention

We keep no personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Game data stays on the device until the game is uninstalled. Google retains limited ad-related data under its own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the US COPPA rule or Türkiye's KVKK), you may have the right to ask what data is processed about you or your child, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because the game sends us nothing, these requests are mostly answered by deleting the game. For ad data processed by Google, see Google's privacy controls. You can contact us at any time with questions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
